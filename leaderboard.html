Evaluation scope

The full evaluation comprises 13 benchmark entries and 6,297 cases. The public data release contains 12 subsets and 4,768 cases, excluding BehaviorChain.

Mistakes, HiToM and τ-USI are additional OOD tests and are excluded from Avg. here.
